## Inconsistencies found (code, panel, docs)

1. **Panel unit of named maps (FIXED by the coordinator, entries rewritten).** The Studio used to show `/shop/rarity_stat_ppm/*` and `/wear/rough_ppm/*` as raw ppm; after the unit fix they are shown in percent, and the 6 entries (5 rarities + rough_ppm) now speak in percent (100 % = no change, 122 %, 200 %; range "above 0 %" and "at least 100 %").
2. **Odds and guarantees are Plain, other `_ppm` crate numbers are %.** (consistent with the code; just noting that `odds/*` are per-mille weights, not percents, and the sum is free: the help says the chance is weight / sum.)
3. **No check that the two bot lists stay in step.** Only `FieldProfile` throws (`InvalidArgumentException`) when `count(reward_ppm_by_position) != count(offsets_ppm) + 1`, at run freeze time; the schema validates each list on its own and I found no world invariant or publication gate for it. Editing numbers in the Studio cannot change the list lengths, but "Zasady jako tekst" can (the help warns about it).
4. **Web hard-codes the unserviceable threshold.** `web/src/components/PartCondition.vue` marks "unserviceable" at `percent < 20`; the rule `wear.unserviceable_ppm` (20% today) only drives the breakdown chance. Changing the rule makes the mark in the game wrong. (The `unserviceable_ppm` tip says so.)
5. **Schema text out of date.** The description of `wear` in `rules.schema.json` still mentions `boost_ppm` ("with a booster on it (boost_ppm, ...)"), which does not exist any more (replaced by `load_ppm_per_point`). `power_exponent_ppm` and `stage_ppm` have no schema description; `wear/breakdown` description is correct.
6. **Docs arithmetic.** `docs/api-runs.md` (section on wear) says "5% of the life of tires is 1 539 laps of Zlomowo"; with `life_km.tires` 40 000 and 2.6 km laps it is 769 laps (1 539 would match 80 000 km). The help uses the computed 15 400 laps for a whole life of the tires.
7. **README out of date.** `content/README.md` line 133 still says `tier_price_ppm` is "tiers 1 to 7 unchanged, from 8 +9%"; `starter.json` and the later paragraph have {1: 100%, 7: 163%, 8: 199%}. The help follows starter.json.
8. **`tier_price_ppm` has no effect in the shop today**: crate slots (tires, suspension, brakes, exhaust) have parts up to tier 3 only; the factors for tiers 7 and 8 only matter for the economy simulator (anchors) until such parts exist. The help says so.
9. `wear/rough_ppm` and `wear/life_km` are keyed by SLOT of the game model (`rules/model`), so the star can be a slot someone adds to the model; `PackValidator` rejects names that are not slots. `life_km` is mandatory for a slot that has parts; `rough_ppm` is optional (missing = same as asphalt).
